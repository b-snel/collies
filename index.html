Collie at Dusk

Pen the whole flock before the sun goes down. Miss any, and the coyotes come out to grab them. Chase them down, rescue every sheep, and get them home. Old Hank the shepherd ambles along behind you, shouting the commands.

WASD / ArrowsMove. Sheep run away from you, so get behind them. ShiftRun. SpaceBark. Scatters sheep and scares coyotes. EPounce. A flying leap that knocks coyotes head over tail and makes them drop a sheep. CLie down. The flock bunches up and keeps drifting the way it's going.
Bone super herder Apple basket sheep follow you Lantern coyotes keep back Golden sheep worth 50 Clover patches (from day 2) distract hungry sheep. Get close or bark to snap them out of it. Big ram sometimes charges you. Bark when it paws the ground, or dodge. At night it charges coyotes too.
