The music is synthesized in the browser.

- Sheep bleats, coyote howls, whistles, thunder and other effects are synthesized in the browser.

## Hank's voice
Hank's lines are synthesized speech, not a recording of a person saying them. They were generated with [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) (MIT license) using the `en_GB-vctk-medium` voice, speaker p241 (a Scottish speaker from Perth), pitched up slightly for a cheerier sound. That voice was trained on the **CSTR VCTK Corpus**, Centre for Speech Technology Research, University of Edinburgh ([datashare](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443)), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). If a clip hasn't loaded, the game falls back to the browser's built-in speech.
