# Audio credits

All recordings below are free to use. The game's own code and art are original.

## Music
The music is synthesized in the browser.

## Sound effects
- Dog bark: cut from **"simple_dog_loop"** ([Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/simple_dog_loop)), CC0 1.0.
- Coyote howls and yips: recorded for the game by its creator.
- Sheep bleats, duck quacks, Rascal's yips, the cat's hiss, splashes, the shepherd's-whistle upgrade, thunder and other effects are synthesized in the browser.

## Hank's voice
Hank's whistles are his own: come bye, away to me, walk up, lie down and that'll do, recorded by Hank with Sage barking along. The barks were taken out by following the whistle's pitch and keeping only that, so the notes and timing are exactly as he whistled them. "That'll do. That'll do." at the end of a day is Hank too.

The rest of Hank's lines are synthesized speech, not a recording of a person saying them. They were generated with [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) (MIT license) using the `en_GB-vctk-medium` voice, speaker p241 (a Scottish speaker from Perth), pitched up slightly for a cheerier sound. That voice was trained on the **CSTR VCTK Corpus**, Centre for Speech Technology Research, University of Edinburgh ([datashare](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443)), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). If a clip hasn't loaded, the game falls back to the browser's built-in speech.
