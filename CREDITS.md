# Audio credits

All recordings below are free to use. The game's own code and art are original.

## Music
- **"Scotch Bagpipe Medley No. 1"** and **"Scotch Bagpipe Medley No. 2"**, Lovat Bagpipe Band of New York, recorded 20 November 1915. Library of Congress National Jukebox, via the Internet Archive ([No. 1](https://archive.org/details/loc-jukebox-15540-scotch-bagpipe-medley-no-1), [No. 2](https://archive.org/details/loc-jukebox-15542-scotch-bagpipe-medley-no-2)). Public Domain Mark 1.0. Trimmed, de-hissed and level-matched for the game.

## Sound effects
- Dog barks: **"simple_dog_loop"** ([Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/simple_dog_loop)), CC0 1.0; and **"Dog Barking No. 1"** by D. D. Teoli Jr. A.C. ([Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/dog-barking-no.-1-d.-d.-teoli-jr.-a.-c.)), Public Domain Mark 1.0.
- Sheep: **Soay sheep, Eifelzoo** field recording by Matthes, Radio Aporee ([Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/aporee_28756_33123)), Public Domain Mark 1.0.
- Coyote howls, whistles, thunder and other effects are synthesized in the browser.

## Hank's voice
Hank's lines are synthesized speech, not a recording of a person saying them. They were generated with [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) (MIT license) using the `en_GB-vctk-medium` voice, speaker p237 (a Scottish speaker from Fife). That voice was trained on the **CSTR VCTK Corpus**, Centre for Speech Technology Research, University of Edinburgh ([datashare](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443)), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). If a clip hasn't loaded, the game falls back to the browser's built-in speech.
